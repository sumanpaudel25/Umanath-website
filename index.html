About Me

A Little About My Story

Namaste! I am Umanath Paudel, known fondly to friends, colleagues and students as Sunil Paudel. For more than a decade, I have stood at the blackboard, chalk in hand, helping young minds fall in love with numbers, logic and knowledge.

I currently teach at Holy Cross Bal Adarsha Bidhya Sadan, where I've been a part of the family since 2014. Before that, I spent four wonderful years shaping students at Rising Nepal Secondary Boarding School. I believe every child carries a spark of brilliance — my job is simply to help it catch fire.

Words I Live By

Shlokas That Shape My Teaching

Ancient wisdom that guides a modern classroom.

🚩 गुरु मन्त्र

गुरुर्ब्रह्मा गुरुर्विष्णुः गुरुर्देवो महेश्वरः। गुरुः साक्षात् परब्रह्म तस्मै श्री गुरवे नमः॥

"The Guru is Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva — verily the Supreme Being. Salutations to that Guru."

🪔 विद्या सूत्र

सा विद्या या विमुक्तये।

"That alone is true knowledge which liberates" — education should free the mind, not just fill it.

📜 अनुशासन श्लोक

विद्या ददाति विनयं विनयाद् याति पात्रताम्। पात्रत्वात् धनमाप्नोति धनात् धर्मं ततः सुखम्॥

"Knowledge gives discipline, discipline brings worthiness" — a reminder I share with every student.

🔔 प्रकाश प्रार्थना

असतो मा सद्गमय। तमसो मा ज्योतिर्गमय। मृत्योर्मा अमृतं गमय॥

"Lead me from untruth to truth, from darkness to light" — the true purpose of a teacher.

🪷 सरस्वती वन्दना

या कुन्देन्दुतुषारहारधवला या शुभ्रवस्त्रावृता।

Saraswati Vandana — a salutation to the Goddess of Knowledge, invoked before every lesson begins.

💡 विद्या धनम्

न चोरहार्यं न च राजहार्यं न भ्रातृभाज्यं न च भारकारि। व्यये कृते वर्धत एव नित्यं विद्याधनं सर्वधनप्रधानम्॥

"Knowledge cannot be stolen, divided or burdensome — and it grows the more you share it." The gift I try to give every day.
